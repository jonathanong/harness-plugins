# Eval cases

One row per case: what it checks and its current score. The roll-up for people is in
[README.md](README.md); every run is in [HISTORY.md](HISTORY.md).

The table is generated from [cases.csv](cases.csv) by `scripts/eval-docs.py`; do not edit it.

A score is the mean over a case's attempts, and an attempt's score is the share of its graders
that passed. A priority sets the pass rate a case must reach: critical 80%, high 70%, medium 60%,
low 50%, so 3 of 3 for the first two and 2 of 3 for the others. "Passed" below three attempts,
such as "2 of 2", is a case that stopped once its verdict was settled; read it as the verdict,
not as a rate. "Loaded" is the attempts in which the skill loaded, "Passed when loaded" the passes among those,
and "Passed" the attempts whose answer was right over all attempts; the verdict is on "Passed".
A row with "n/a" under "Loaded" has no `skill-fired` grader, or was measured before 2026-11-26,
when "Passed" also required the skill to load. "Guard:" marks a case where the skill must stay out of the way; the model passes it
unaided by design.
